Official Privacy Policy

VoiceSphere Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 22, 2026

VoiceSphere ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how data is handled. VoiceSphere is an inclusive voice community platform providing real-time WebRTC audio rooms, assistive speech communication (AAC), and accessibility tools.

1. Information We Collect and Process

  • Ephemeral Audio Data: When you join a voice room and enable your microphone, your voice is transmitted directly via encrypted WebRTC streams to other room participants. We do not record, store, or archive your audio conversations to disk or servers. Audio streams exist only in volatile memory during live room transmission.
  • Room Metadata & Temporary Profiles: Temporary room identifiers, display nicknames, participant lists, and live captions generated during an active session are maintained only while the room is active.
  • Local Device Settings: Accessibility preferences (high-contrast themes, sensory pacing, AAC symbol selections) are stored locally on your device via standard local storage.

2. Android Permissions Used

  • RECORD_AUDIO (Microphone): Required strictly to allow you to speak in live voice rooms. Microphone access is requested only when you actively join a room or initiate speech input.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to establish WebRTC peer-to-peer and SFU connections and sync live room statuses.

3. Advertising & Google AdMob

VoiceSphere displays advertisements through the official Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to support the hosting and maintenance of our free accessibility infrastructure:

  • Google AdMob may collect standard device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address, and app interaction data to serve ads.
  • In applicable regions (European Economic Area, UK, Switzerland, and California), user consent for personalized advertising is gathered via the official Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).
  • Advertisements are strictly suppressed during active voice calls to prevent audio disruption.
  • For more details on how Google uses information from apps, visit Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

VoiceSphere is intended for general audiences and adults (High Maturity rating, age 18 and older). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Data Security & Storage

All communication between your device and our servers is secured using modern TLS/HTTPS encryption. WebRTC audio transmission uses DTLS-SRTP end-to-end transport security.
